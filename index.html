About

How I work

Most of my career has been spent on systems where a missed defect is expensive — federal applications at USDA and Treasury, defense programs at Raytheon, payment processing at Wells Fargo. That shapes how I test: full traceability from requirement to test case, automation that people actually trust, and documentation someone else can pick up.

I work in Agile teams as a matter of routine — Scrum and Kanban, sprint planning through retrospective — and I'm comfortable being the person who defines the QA process rather than inheriting one. I've built data-driven, keyword-driven, and hybrid frameworks from the ground up, and mentored engineers on using them.
